## Independent checks and limits

- The 650 frozen liked rows contain 477 Deezer IDs, zero ISRCs, zero preview hashes and zero verified add timestamps. Grouping the observed Deezer IDs across the position cutoff gives five held-out rows sharing five IDs with six older rows. The 306 score rows contain 305 distinct Deezer IDs. Neither within-panel repetition nor historical fit contamination implies that every arm was helped by the same amount. Further alias discovery requires the actual match or preview cache. [Feature rows](../features_v2.json) · [Score rows](../v3_scores_306.json)
- The v4 manifest joined same-ISRC, same-preview and transitive bridge fixtures across providers. Two excluded training twins were given different feature vectors. The seven remaining training likes, four training rejects, selected seed and all v4 scored pools were identical on both runs. Disjoint reject folds and rejection of conflicting labels also passed. These tests check the grouping algorithm with observed keys. Authenticity or completeness of real external identity mappings remains unverified. [Recording split](../evaluate_v4.py) · [Refit](../integrate_v4.py) · [Regression fixture](../test_audit_metrics.py)
- Standard full-panel NDCG@10 on the historical 306 rows equals .471036 for v2, .368658 for v6 per-bucket, .713654 for Troi, .603262 for full Embeat, and .689829 for Embeat minus channel 1. Calculating DCG from the ordered binary relevance and comparing against sklearn produced the same values. For the stored score columns the source order resolves equal v6 scores. Cosine tie-break values for the deep Embeat tail are absent. Every Embeat top-ten item has a unique score. [Complete row reconstruction](recomputation.md)
- On these stored rows the corrected Troi-minus-v6 sensitivity run with 10,000 common draws, seed 42 and original point tie orders yielded standard NDCG percentiles [−.1540, .7846] and precision percentiles [−.10, .80]. The observed full-panel NDCG difference was .344996. At the predeclared checkpoints, independent exact-binomial and Wilson calculations gave 32/42 a one-sided p of .021125 and lower bound .614722, and 37/50 a p of .027988 and lower bound .604466. These fixed-pool and fixed-count calculations cover different sampling units. [Ranking tests](../test_troi_patch.py) · [Gate tests](../test_evaluate_v4.py)
