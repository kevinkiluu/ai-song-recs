# Ranked enhancement plan

v2 remains the production ranker. The fresh Embeat channel-1 comparison still has insufficient evidence. Older score tables, current playlist membership, and fixtures answer different questions. Each measurement below uses a fixed cohort and a declared decision rule.

| Priority | Evidence gap | Falsifiable claim | Measurement and decision |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | The fresh gate needs 10 eligible liked recordings and 2 eligible rejects. The published 9-like shortfall depends on one eligible like. The frozen 590-row catalog, generation-time exposures and corrected fresh judgment join are missing here. | Replaying the original sources recovers the claimed 1 eligible like and 11 rejects. Nine additional unique eligible likes would then clear the count gate. | Recover the original hashes, exposures, two liked exports and catalog crosswalk. Publish counts for offered, kept, left, removed, joined, conflicting, already known, and eligible. Use one row per recording. For later data, count only judgments acquired after freezing the challenger, without reusing these observations for tuning. Recalculate `max(0,10−eligible_likes)` and `max(0,2−eligible_rejects)`. Passing the counts only permits evaluation. |
| 2 | Five held-out preview IDs also occur in six training rows. The 306-row historical audit also contains two copies of one held-out recording. Cached liked rows have no ISRC or byte hashes for an independent identity check. | A recording-level split and fold-local fitting eliminate cross-partition matches and keep one evaluation row per recording. | Restore Deezer matches and authorized preview hashes, then build connected components across Spotify ID, Deezer ID, ISRC and preview hash before fitting. On each fold require zero component overlap across train/test and unique evaluation components, and report coverage of each identity field. Rerun v2 and both Embeat arms on the same eligible rows. Report metrics and excluded counts before/after. Fixes here cover the split; the data run is outstanding. |
| 3 | ReccoBeats is present for 447/650 likes (68.8%), 33/130 newest (25.4%), and 414/520 older (79.6%). A strong old-cohort result can hide an imputation problem for recent likes. | Verified Spotify-to-ISRC matching recovers additional recent features and improves ranking of newer likes. | Preserve the original cache. On a separate versioned cache, count newly matched recent recordings with independently agreeing identities and all usable values. Compare coverage for the same 130 and held-out P@10 and NDCG@10 on a fixed pool with the same split and model. Report coverage gains separately from rank deltas. |
| 4 | 173/650 rows have no Deezer ID; 175 lack usable preview features. Of all likes, 55 have neither source. Existing cache errors can survive a recovered download. | Retrying failed requests by matched recording ID restores usable features for at least one formerly missing recording. | On a separate cache, partition missing rows into no match, matched/no preview, transfer failure and decode failure. Record successful redecodes and count how many of the fixed 55 gain any source. Rerun paired scoring on fixed shared coverage and report how many extra recordings become evaluable. Unchanged counts falsify the recovery claim. |
| 5 | The historical Bliss/v2 blend scores .538886 standard NDCG on the 306-row sample. A sampled/full-pool reversal was reported, but the full score vectors and original pool are absent. | The reported reversal persists when the full pool and all seeded draws share the same scores, labels, metric and tie rule. | Score the full eligible pool once. Preserve its recording IDs, score columns and hash. Slice the five predeclared draws from these rows. Publish P@10 and standard NDCG@10 deltas versus the same per-bucket baseline for every draw and the full pool, plus each top-ten entrant/displaced recording. A sign difference reproduces the disagreement; equal signs falsify it for these draws. Keep the full pool primary. |
| 6 | The streaming-history download check is scheduled for Nov 26. The liked export has no verified save timestamps, and an unsaved play by itself supplies no dislike label. | A history-based signal adds predictive value beyond the fixed v2 model on later, judged recordings. | On Nov 26 check export availability. If received, measure valid Spotify ID and timestamp coverage, join rate, exposure counts and repeated plays, and predefine skip/completion handling. Train strictly before each time boundary. Evaluate changes in P@10, NDCG@10 and known rejects in the top ten on the same later judged recordings. Estimate uncertainty across independent time blocks when enough exist. Until then keep streaming-based estimates pending. |
